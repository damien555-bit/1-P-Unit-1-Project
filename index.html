Allosaurus Fragilis

Overview

Allosaurus fragilis is a species of carnivorous dinosaur that existed 155-100 million years ago.
First discovered by Othniel Charles Marsh in 1877, it is a well known theropod and known as one of the distinctive apex predators of the Jurassic period.
Allosaurus fossil specimens can be found:

  • On the fourth floor of the American Museum of Natural History, in the Hall of Saurischian Dinosaurs.
    Two fossils are featured, a skull and a more complete body
