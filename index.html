Allosaurus Fragilis

Overview

Allosaurus fragilis is a species of carnivorous dinosaur that existed 155-100 million years ago.
First discovered by Othniel Charles Marsh in 1877, it is a well known theropod and known as one of the distinctive apex predators of the Jurassic period.

Allosaurus fossil specimens on display can be found:

  • The American Museum of Natural History, in the Hall of Saurischian Dinosaurs, and in the Rotunda/lobby.


  • The Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History .



  • The Natural History Museum of Utah. Many different specimens can be found here, since it is the state with the most reported allosaurus fossils found.

Allosaurus has also been featured in movies like Jurassic World: Dominion, and the short film "Battle at Big Rock".
